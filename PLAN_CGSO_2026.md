## 2. Why Team B was probably rejected

Zharief didn't say why, but the drafts show it:

1. **No theme (the main reason).** None of the 6 shows haze, document security or the economy. They're generic patriotic collages that remix 2024/2025.
2. **Mandatory elements missing.** No draft has research. `CGSO.pdf` p3 has no Istana Negara at all. `CGSO.pdf` p1–p3 have no rakyat.
3. **Recycled assets.** `CGSO (1).pdf` p2 reuses the same children from the 2025 cover. The client will notice.
4. **Off-tone imagery.** `CGSO.pdf` p2 shows two officers in a fight pose with a shouting officer, which is aggressive for a government book. p3 uses a robot-hand cliché and makes the Petronas Twin Towers (a commercial landmark) the hero.
5. **Execution.** Cutouts look like stickers, with no shared lighting or shadows. The flag texture is pixelated (`CGSO.pdf` p2). The children look AI-generated (`CGSO (1).pdf` p1). The hibiscus is pasted on top. There's no focal point.
6. **Spec errors.** `CGSO.pdf` p3 is 130 × 205 mm, not A5. Its grunge roundel changes the official tagline mark.
7. **Copied typography.** The yellow + white bold sans is the same as 2025.

**How we win:** each cover tells its theme within 3 seconds, every mandatory element is present, and the execution is clean and premium. We keep the approved layout system so the cover still belongs to the series.

### De-AI finishing pass (per cover, in Photoshop)
The composite has to read as one photograph taken by one camera. Do these in order:
1. **Match tones.** Clip a Curves layer to each element and match its black point, white point and midtone colour to the plate behind it.
2. **Make cutouts sit in the scene.** Give every cutout a contact shadow and an ambient shadow that agree with the concept's light direction, plus a 2–4 px light wrap from the background colour on the lit edges. Never add a glowing halo.
3. **Add depth.** Put a little atmospheric haze on distant elements, a slight lens blur on the far background, and keep the focus on the hero.
4. **Check skin.** Pull back orange saturation. Never smooth skin. If a face looks waxy, add Camera Raw Texture +10 to +15 on that layer.
5. **Grade once.** Put one Camera Raw (or Color Grading + Curves) layer over everything, so the whole cover shares a single grade.
6. **Unify the grain.** Add a 50% grey layer in Overlay mode with Add Noise 2–3% (monochromatic, Gaussian), blurred 0.3 px. This is the single biggest de-AI step: one shared grain ties every element together.
7. **Sharpen once,** at the end: High Pass 1 px in Soft Light at 30–50% opacity. Don't sharpen layers individually.
8. **Final check** at 100% and 200% against the AI-tell list.

### `02_Vectors/Flag/`: exact Jalur Gemilang (verified: 14 stripes starting red, canton = 8 stripes × ½ length, 14-point star, 1:2)
To put it on the AI fabric (S-1):
1. Duplicate the fabric, desaturate it, apply Gaussian Blur 2–3 px, and save it as `displace.psd`.
2. Place `Jalur_Gemilang_6000x3000.png` over the fabric and warp it roughly to the fabric's outline.
3. Run Filter → Distort → Displace (10 / 10, Stretch to Fit, Repeat Edge) with `displace.psd`.
4. Clip the flag to the fabric shape and set it to Multiply. Put a copy of the fabric above it in Soft Light so the highlights come through.
5. Use the `_REVERSE` file only when the pole (hoist) is on the **right**.

### `02_Vectors/C2_Security/`
- `C2_guilloche_rosette`: 140 mm rosette with two microtext rings (tagline + headline; office name). Centre it behind the Istana's main dome at 120–140 mm wide, Normal or Screen at 35–60% opacity, and mask it where it passes behind people.
- `C2_guilloche_wavefield`: full-cover security-paper line field. Use it at 8–15% opacity, mainly in the lower half.
- `C2_microtext_frame`: optional border, 8 mm inside the trim with the corners kept clear, at 50–70% opacity.
- Each comes as SVG/PDF (vector) and PNG (rosette 600 ppi, others 300 ppi), in gold `#C9A227`. Recolour with Color Overlay if needed.

### `02_Vectors/C3_Growth/`
- `C3_rising_line`: a tapered metallic-gold growth line with trend hairlines and data-point nodes. It's pre-positioned on the canvas to clear the roundel and headline; place the PNG at 0,0.
- `C3_protective_arc`: a double arc that forms a dome over the Istana.
- Glow comes as separate `_glow` PNGs: set them to Screen at 40–70%. Keep the glow restrained so it doesn't read as a stock light streak. The SVGs are editable in Illustrator.
