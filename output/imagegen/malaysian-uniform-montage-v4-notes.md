## Direction and evidence

The new design direction depicts UTK alongside regular PDRM personnel. This is a design choice based on the PDRM brief and maroon berets, not identification of the original generated men. The commercial green garment is not established as a PDRM uniform. Official PDRM photos show blue-grey digital camouflage, POLIS tapes, Malaysian flag patches, and PDRM beret crests. These references supersede the commercial garment for this accuracy-focused version. The supplied faces and three-panel composition were retained as closely as the image editor allowed.
